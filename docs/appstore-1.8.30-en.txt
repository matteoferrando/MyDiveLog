Dive computers, one by one: the library that reads them is updated, and the connection has been rechecked family by family.

TEN NEW MODELS

Perdix 3, Quad 2, Sirius L, Puck Pro EZ, Puck Pro Ultra, Raffaello, Seac Tablet, OSTC 3, OSTC cR and OSTC Nano connect over Bluetooth. And the library brings fixes for computers that already downloaded: Halcyon with the newest protocol, Mares with a skewed memory pointer.

AQUALUNG i330R

If a download had stopped halfway, on the next connection the computer sent the tail of the old read before answering, and the app took it for the answer. Now it listens before speaking and discards what cannot be an answer. And if the computer was reset or paired with another phone, it asks for the new PIN by itself instead of stopping.

DIVES ARE READ WITH THE REAL MODEL

For many computers the model decides how the data is read. Until now the one picked from the list counted; now the one the computer declares when it connects does, and if the pick was wrong the name on the dives is corrected.

THE RIGHT MARES

A device announcing "Mares bluelink pro" chooses among the adapter's models, one with built-in Bluetooth among its own: a Puck Pro Ultra is no longer offered as the earlier-generation Puck Pro, which speaks a different packet shape.

PLANNER

On closed circuit every gas except the diluent can be declared "bailout". Under the bailout cylinder's consumption the wrong gas name appeared: fixed.

AND ALSO

The gradient factor slider is finger-sized. On iPhone and Mac the system buttons inside the app follow your language. Vertical speed and the decompression line draw correctly even when the file's samples are out of order.
