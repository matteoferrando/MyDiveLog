Four fixes found by an independent review of the code. None produced an error: they happened silently.

• All-or-nothing saving: if the system kills the app mid-save, the dive is in the logbook whole with its profile, or not at all. Before, it could be left without its profile.

• Sync no longer shortens an already-saved profile (measured: 100 samples cut to 5).

• After an import, nitrogen loading now shows up straight away, with no need to reopen the app.
